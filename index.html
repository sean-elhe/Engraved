How to Use Engraved

Engraved is a Bible memorization app designed to help you internalize Scripture through active recall and spaced repetition. Here's how to get started:

1. Select Your Passage

Tap the gear icon in the top left to open the settings menu. From there, you can choose the book, chapter, translation, and difficulty level you'd like to practice.

2. Practice Your Memory

Once you've selected your passage, you'll be presented with the first verse. Tap on the blanks to fill in the missing words. There is a hint button if you need a little help! You first need to have a blank selected, after a few seconds the option for a hint will come up,

3. Track Your Progress

As you practice, Engraved will keep track of your progress and provide feedback on your performance. After completing a chapter, you'll receive a score and a breakdown of your results.

Happy memorizing!
