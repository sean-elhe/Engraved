Information

Welcome to Engraved! Select a book and chapter from settings, then tap the blanks to fill in the answers!

Controls & Gestures

Forward Button (or Enter key): • Tap: Submits answers for the current verse.
• Long-Press: Submits the whole chapter instantly.
Back Button: • Tap: Resets text entered for the current verse.
• Long-Press: Restarts the chapter, you'll begin from the beginning with a fresh score.
Hint Function: Click a blank target word first to focus it, then tap the hint button to reveal a character.

Profile Storage

  • User accounts: Create an account using a username and a simple 4-digit PIN (e.g., last 4 digits of your phone number).
  • Bookmarks: Long-press the heart icon to bookmark the current chapter.
  • Saved scores: Click the save button at the end of the score screen to save your current score.

Mode Mechanics

Difficulty levels: Easy conceals 20% of words, Medium conceals 40%, and Hard conceals 60%. This excludes words under 3 characters, which are excluded from being hidden.

Verse orders: Sequential loads verses in the natural order. Randomized shuffles all verses in the chapter, while ensuring that every verse will be shown only once as well .
